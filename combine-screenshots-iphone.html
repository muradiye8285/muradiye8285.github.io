Taking multiple screenshots on iPhone is easy, but sharing them one by one can feel messy.

Why combine screenshots?

This is useful when you want to show a full conversation, long instructions, product details, or multiple steps in one image.

Common use cases

  • Sharing step-by-step tutorials
  • Posting cleaner content online
