How to Remove Metadata from Photos on iPhone

Most people don’t realize that photos taken on an iPhone contain hidden data.

This data can include the exact location where the photo was taken.

What is metadata?

Metadata (EXIF data) includes GPS location, time, and device details.

Why remove it?

Sharing photos without removing metadata can expose personal information.
