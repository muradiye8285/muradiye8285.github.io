How to Remove Location from Photos on iPhone

Quick fix: Remove location data before sharing your photos.

When you take a photo on your iPhone, it often includes hidden location data (GPS). Most people don’t notice this, but when you share a photo, that data can go with it.

This means someone could potentially see where the photo was taken — your home, workplace, or a private location.

What is location data in photos?

Location data is part of something called metadata (EXIF). It stores information like:

  • GPS coordinates
  • Date and time
  • Device information

On iPhone, this is added automatically when Location Services is enabled for the Camera.

Why you should remove location data

Sharing photos without removing location data can expose personal information. This is especially important if you:

  • Post photos on social media
  • Send images to people you don’t fully trust
  • Share screenshots or private moments

How to remove location from photos on iPhone (step-by-step)

  1. Open the Photos app
  2. Select the photo you want to share
  3. Swipe up or tap the (i) info icon
  4. Look for the map / location section
  5. Tap "Adjust" or remove the location

This works for individual photos, but it’s not always fast if you have multiple images.

Better way: remove location before sharing

A more reliable method is to remove location data before sending or uploading photos. This ensures no hidden data is accidentally shared.

Using a dedicated tool makes this process faster and more consistent, especially when handling multiple images.

When you should always remove location

  • Before posting on Instagram, Twitter, or forums
  • Before sharing screenshots publicly
  • When sending images to unknown people
  • When sharing photos from home or personal spaces
