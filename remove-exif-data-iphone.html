Every photo taken on an iPhone contains hidden information called EXIF data.

This includes location, time, device info, and more.

What is EXIF data?

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) stores metadata inside your photos.

  • GPS location
  • Date and time
  • Camera details

Why remove EXIF data?

Sharing photos with EXIF data can expose private information.
